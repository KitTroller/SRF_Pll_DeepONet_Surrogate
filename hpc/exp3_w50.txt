# Fill the gap in the W curve. F32 found a flat bottom at W=40-100; W=50 is the ONLY
# divisor of N=5000 between them (5000 = 2^3 * 5^4), so this is the whole gap.
#
# famB_W50.npz is DERIVED from famB_W10.npz by src/rewindow.py, not regenerated --
# S=100 divides S=500, so the samples are bit-identical and the LHS family is preserved.
# Regenerating would have drawn a new (unseeded) LHS and voided the comparison.
#   python src/rewindow.py famB_W10.npz --W 50
#
# 4 seeds, matching every other point in the W sweep.
--dataset famB_W50.npz --F 4 --max_freq 503 --w_phys 0.3 --seed 0 --split_seed 0 --epochs 800 --patience 40 --n_eval_runs 150 --results_dir sweeps_famB_W
