# and its tie with W=40 is a lower bound.
#
# The two hypotheses are far apart here, which makes this a clean discriminator:
#   basis (2*pi/T)          -> 1257 rad/s at W=100 (5 ms window)
#   fixed absolute (F20)    ->  503 rad/s, unchanged
# F20 rejected the basis reading at W=20 and mf503 tied mf628 at W=40, so "fixed
# absolute" is the standing reading -- but it has never been tested where the two
# predictions differ by 2.5x. If 1257 wins here, F20 is wrong and the W sweep needs
# redoing with mf tuned per W.
#
