turns off  = runs whose FINAL error is a whole multiple of 2*pi away, i.e. the
             surrogate counted cycle slips differently from the reference. That
             is a discrete failure worth 2*pi each, not a loss of precision.
ours/ctrl  = degradation of the surrogate vs its in-distribution control
solv/ctrl  = how much harder the scenario is to INTEGRATE at all -- the part
             that is physics, not extrapolation
ours/solv  = the surrogate's excess over the floor at its own step. This is
             the number that isolates extrapolation: if it stays near the
             control's value the operator is still doing its job, whatever
             happened to the absolute error.
